## Main post

Been building something in my spare time: BMS, a small invoicing app for small businesses.

It started as a rough little address book and I took it all the way. Real invoices with line items, tax and multiple currencies. A dashboard with revenue charts. Customer histories. PDF export. Dark mode, keyboard shortcuts, and it works on your phone.

The fun part: no backend at all. Everything runs in the browser, your data stays in your browser.

Try the live demo: https://vrdevil44.github.io/BMS-project/

## First comment (tech details)

For the curious: Next.js 13, TypeScript (strict), Tailwind. Fully client-side with a clean data layer, so it can plug into a real backend later. Type-safe, unit-tested, lint-clean. Code's public: https://github.com/Vrdevil44/BMS-project
